## Output foundation
A single evidence dossier powers multiple renderings: full research report, short executive insight, chart pack, newsletter/media script, presenter-ready visual briefing, video, and optional research-grounded original sci-fi film.

## Dossier sections
Question and scope; executive answer; key quantified findings with source/date; evidence tables and provenance; contradictions; key drivers; methods/data gaps; conditional pathways/probabilities only if validated; monitoring signals; references and publication/version fingerprint. Deliver machine-readable provenance for claims and visual assets.

## Presenter Edition 3–5 minutes
- B2B licensed graphics/video with **timed cue sheet**, timed script, teleprompter version, editor notes, precise timestamp-to-claim links and rights manifest.
- Use verifiable footage, original charts/maps and labeled reconstructions; never portray synthetic visuals as documentary footage of real events.
- Q&A and alternative hypotheses for presenter, on-screen disclosure and human editorial approval. No false quotes attributed to public officials.

## MANARA Originals — future films
20-minute research-inspired science-fiction shorts *as optional target, not initial requirement*; premise anchored in plausible scientific advances, authored characters and continuity via Film Bible, shot/asset prompts, dialogue/music rights, consistent shot list, montage and QA. Free film for awareness; premium deeper research report, educational/license packs. Fiction must be clearly fictional and free scientific caveat/context kept accessible.

## Commercial model
Research packs, newsroom licenses, institutional intelligence subscriptions, custom research, SDK/API, educational formats; pricing/volume are hypotheses requiring validation. Original rights and third-party data licensing separately checked per asset and source; consent for likeness/audio; no redistribution by default.

## Quality gates
Claim citation maps, visual factual consistency, script timing, captions/RTL and accessible output, copyright/rights review, versioned report snapshots, editorial review for high-stakes claims. Film assembly integration later, never block initial core validation.
