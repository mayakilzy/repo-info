## Product promise versus evidence
Foresight = conditional pathways, quantified forecasts only if valid; not fortune-telling or deterministic predictions. Specialized science/economy/politics questions require distinct data and validity rules. Missing evidence must be a first-class reportable outcome.

## Central Forecast Router
Classify target/outcome, horizon, geographic scope, data history, revision and leakage risks. Choose among: trend/ETS/ARIMA/baseline time series; hierarchical market adoption; calibrated probability models where justified; Bayesian evidence updates; technology readiness and diffusion scenarios; structured expert-judgment scenario analysis; or **insufficient evidence**. Domain-specific validated plugin methods via typed contract; no one predictor for every sector.

## Minimum evidence standard
Define measurable outcome and date. Snapshot input data cutoff; no future leakage. Compare against naive and robust baselines, backtest across multiple historical cutoffs, measure MAE/RMSE for quantities, Brier/log loss and calibration curves for probabilities. Explicit assumption and scenario sensitivity. Probability estimates without empirical basis are prohibited. No unearned numerical precision.

## Temporal and audit contracts
Forecast record immutable at publication (issue date, horizon, metric/unit, reference region, input dataset hashes, model/method version, probability distribution when verified, baseline and evaluation protocol); subsequent revisions link to original as new entries. Track incorrect as well as correct outcomes. Source timestamps must be before cutoff in historical replay.

## Scientific maturity ladder
Paper/lab claim ≠ independent replication ≠ prototype ≠ safety validation ≠ regulation ≠ factory scale ≠ sale ≠ adoption. Track category-specific gates; an announcement is evidence an announcement occurred, not proof it will be delivered.

## Cases
Battery cost per kWh: battery pack vs cell separation, inflation and region; test published series. Prosthetics adoption: clinical evidence, reimbursement, regulatory approval, affordability; often scenario-only. Political risk: competing actors/narratives, uncertain causal pathways; event corroboration essential. Waste recycling: rates require same denominator and geography; policy changes may break series.

## Deliverables
Method-selection audit, data gaps table, evidence ledger, baseline performance, uncertainty bands where justified, assumptions and alternatives, error analyses, monitoring indicator suggestions, public explanation accessible to non-experts.
