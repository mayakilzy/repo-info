## Vision
MANARA is a globally applicable, sector-agnostic **adaptive intelligence factory**. It transforms an investigation question into discoverable sources, validated search capabilities, evidence-backed structured knowledge, calibrated forecasting/scenarios when possible, and publishable research/visual products.

**Value:** Research the future and help the user see it. An intelligence capability library is the compounding asset—not model-generated prose, not hundreds of duplicated services.

## Personas
- Independent analysts, journalists, newsroom producers, broadcasters, podcasters, YouTubers, science educators, university lecturers, book writers and large editorial blogs.
- Research units, strategic teams, executives, industrial/market analysts, enterprise intelligence API users.
- Curious individuals seeking understandable well-grounded investigations.

## Products
- Evidence-rich investigative dossiers, analytical briefs, charts and scenario packages.
- 3–5-minute presenter-ready visual intelligence editions with timed script/cue sheets/citations/licensing notes; no staged synthetic news footage masquerading as real.
- Free original research-grounded science-fiction shorts (e.g. future smart farming, medicine, batteries) with **premium depth dossier**. Film bible, storyboard, asset provenance, rights, human review and non-misleading fiction distinction.
- Data/API and bespoke research/licensing where rights permit. Pricing and product-market fit remain hypotheses, not proven commitments.

## Hard requirements
1. One tested shared machine; domain molds are configured specializations, not cloned cores.
2. No-domain and no-search-engine paths are first-class (discover, build, validate, compose temporary mold).
3. Discovery/reuse/build separation, with canonical Source–Connector–Engine identities.
4. Claims are sourced, time-aware and contestable. Contradictions preserved. Forecasts fixed at publication and compared to baselines.
5. Temporary agent runtimes terminate; verified recipes persist and are rehydratable.
6. Information custody, commercial rights, tenant isolation, SSRF protections, no secret leakage and signed/approved promotion.
7. Work pragmatically: small codebase, large capability; reuse standards/Toolkit read-only; thin slices over complex platform upfront.

## Scope distinctions
**V1 architecture and slices:** Source discovery, capability registry, simple build/adaptor, evidence and investigation, scenario-capable forecasting policy, agent/mold recipes and one complete real pilot. **Later:** Autonomous continuous horizon scanning, mature film studio, rich customer billing, licensed distribution and optional third-party capability marketplace.

## Explicit non-goals
No magical reliable prediction of every domain, no all-source unrestricted scraping, no unsupervised generated-code production promotion, no forced probabilities, no auto-accepting scientific claims from promotional releases, no re-training bespoke LLM for every specialty, no default autonomous 24/7 agent farm.
